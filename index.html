07

Method

Data is ERA5 reanalysis from the Copernicus Climate Data Store, 1980–2025, daily temperature and rainfall at roughly 28km resolution. Trend significance is tested with Mann-Kendall and Sen's slope, cross-checked against BEAST Bayesian changepoint detection. Forecasting uses an LSTM neural network and a Random Forest baseline. SHAP values identify which inputs actually drive extreme-event predictions.

A research project, not an operational forecasting service. The 2026–2035 line is a trend projection, not a weather forecast.
